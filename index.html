Three ways to buy the same thing

Same product, very different prices.

New

Sealed, full manufacturer warranty, standard return window. Best for gifts and anything you want pristine.

Open box

Returned or display units checked by the store. Often noticeably cheaper, usually still under warranty. Condition grades vary.

Refurbished

Repaired and tested, by the manufacturer or a third party. The biggest savings, but warranty and quality vary the most.
