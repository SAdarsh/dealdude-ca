    Three ways to buy the same thing.

    Same product, very different prices. Knowing the difference is the easiest way to save.

    New

    Sealed, full manufacturer warranty, standard return window. Best for gifts and anything you want pristine.

    Open box

    Returned or display units checked by the store. Often noticeably cheaper, usually still under warranty.

    Refurbished

    Repaired and tested by the manufacturer or a third party. The biggest savings, with the most variation in warranty.
