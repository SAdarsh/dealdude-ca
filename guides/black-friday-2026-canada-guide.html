Black Friday 2026 in Canada: dates and a game plan

Published November 18, 2026 · Deals Dude team

Black Friday in Canada is no longer one day. Most major retailers start "early Black Friday" promotions in mid-November and keep them going through Cyber Monday. Then Boxing Day brings a second wave. Here is how to make the most of it without overspending.

Key dates for 2026

EventDateWhat to expect
Early Black FridayMid to late NovemberRolling deals, often on the same items that appear on Black Friday itself.
Black FridayFriday, November 27Doorbusters and the widest range of discounts, especially on electronics.
Cyber MondayMonday, November 30Online-focused deals. Some Black Friday prices return or get extended.
Boxing DaySaturday, December 26Canada's other big sale. Often strong for TVs, appliances and open-box clearance.

A simple game plan

  1. Make a short list now. Write down the exact model you want, including storage, size and colour. "A 65-inch TV" is not a plan; a specific model number is.
  2. Note today's price. A screenshot or a note with the date is enough. When the sale hits, you will know if the discount is real.
  3. Check every condition. An open-box or manufacturer-refurbished unit can beat the best "new" Black Friday price, sometimes by a lot.
  4. Compare the total, not the sticker. Add shipping and any environmental handling fees, which vary by province. A lower sticker price can cost more in total.
  5. Read the return policy. Holiday return windows are often extended for new items but can be shorter for open-box or marketplace sellers.
  6. Set a budget and stop. The best deal is the one on something you actually needed.
Tip: Many stores match each other's prices on identical items. If you find a lower price elsewhere, check the store's price-match policy before you buy. Note that Black Friday doorbusters are often excluded.

Black Friday or Boxing Day?

There is no universal answer. Black Friday tends to favour new electronics and gift items. Boxing Day can be better for big-ticket items and for clearing out open-box stock returned after the holidays. If what you want is not discounted on Black Friday, waiting a month can pay off.
