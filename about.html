About

Why Deals Dude?

Shopping for electronics in Canada usually means opening a dozen tabs: the big retailers, the computer stores, the marketplaces, and their separate open-box and refurbished sections. Prices change constantly, especially in November.

Deals Dude puts them side by side: new, open box and refurbished, with price history so you can tell a real deal from a marketing number.

How we work

  • Independent. We are not owned by any retailer. Our guides are written to be useful, not to push a store.
  • Transparent. Some links earn us a commission. That never changes the price you pay, and it never decides which offer we show as cheapest. See our affiliate disclosure.
  • Honest about prices. Prices change quickly. Every price shows when we last checked it, and we always point you to the retailer to confirm.
  • A polite crawler. Our price checker identifies itself as DealsDudeBot, follows each site's robots.txt and keeps its request rate low.
