Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Deals Dude respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is involved when you use this website.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or payment details on this site. Our hosting provider may automatically log technical information, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and operation purposes.

Cookies and local storage

This site stores your light or dark theme preference in your browser's local storage. It is not sent to us. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering the font files.

Affiliate links

When you click a link to a retailer such as Amazon.ca, that retailer may set cookies to attribute your purchase to us. Their handling of your information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Future features

If we add features like price alerts that need your email address, we will ask for your express consent first, in line with Canada's anti-spam legislation (CASL) and PIPEDA. We will update this policy before launching them.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@dealsdude.ca.
