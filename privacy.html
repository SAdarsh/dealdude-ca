Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Deals Dude respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is involved when you use this website.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or payment details on this site. We don't use analytics, advertising trackers or third-party scripts.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), and its domain name service is provided by Cloudflare. As with any website, these providers may automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to deliver the site. Their handling is governed by their own privacy statements.

Cookies and local storage

We don't set cookies. This site stores your light or dark theme preference in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Affiliate links

When you click a link to a retailer such as Amazon.ca, that retailer may set cookies to attribute your purchase to us. Their handling of your information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Future features

If we add features like price alerts that need your email address, we will ask for your express consent first, in line with Canada's anti-spam legislation (CASL) and PIPEDA. We will update this policy before launching them.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your personal information under PIPEDA? Email hello@dealsdude.ca.
